# Application Access Requests

## Overview
Access to business applications and shared folders is granted by role and needs approval from the application owner.

## How to request access
1. Describe the application and the access level you need, for example read-only or editor.
2. Give a short business reason.
3. The request is routed to your manager and then to the application owner for approval.
4. Approved access is normally granted within one working day.

## Common problems
1. "Access denied" after approval: sign out and sign in again so your permissions refresh.
2. You changed teams: your old access is removed automatically and the new access must be requested again.

## Escalation
For urgent access that blocks customer work, raise an incident for Identity and Access Support and mention your manager.
