# Account Unlock

## Overview
Use this article when your account is locked or you are locked out of your account. Accounts lock automatically after five failed sign-in attempts and unlock by themselves after 30 minutes.

## Common causes
1. An old password saved on your phone or laptop keeps retrying in the background.
2. A recently changed password was not updated on all your devices.

## How to unlock
1. Wait 30 minutes for the automatic unlock, or use the password portal to unlock immediately.
2. The helpdesk assistant can unlock the account automatically after checking the directory status.
3. After unlock, remove old saved passwords from your phone mail app and VPN client.

## Escalation
If the account locks again within an hour, raise an incident for Identity and Access Support because a device may be sending wrong passwords.
