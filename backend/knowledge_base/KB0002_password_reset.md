# Password Reset

## Overview
Corporate passwords expire every 90 days. An expired or forgotten password blocks email, VPN and all company applications.

## Self-service reset
1. Open the company password portal from any device.
2. Enter your employee ID and verify your identity with the MFA code.
3. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters, including upper case, lower case, a number and a symbol.
4. You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.
5. Wait two minutes, then sign in again. Update the saved password on your phone and laptop.

## Automated reset by the helpdesk assistant
If the directory confirms that the password is expired, the assistant can start an automated reset. A secure reset link is sent to your registered email. The assistant never shows or stores your password.

## After the reset
Sign out of Outlook, Teams and VPN and sign in again with the new password. If you still cannot sign in, your account may be locked (see Account Unlock).
